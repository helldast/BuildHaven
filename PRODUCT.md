# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (user's choice). No framework.

## Users

Prospective buyers of new apartments in Ostrava – Krásné Pole, Czech Republic. They are weighing a large purchase, comparing units by layout, size and price, and they want to know the developer is real and reachable before they get in touch.

## Product Purpose

BuildHaven s.r.o. is a Czech developer that builds new apartments and sells them directly to buyers. The website presents the development and the company.

Success means a visitor can:
- browse the apartments and see which are available, with floor plans and sizes;
- find contact information easily;
- learn about the company behind the development.

## Positioning

Location and the apartments themselves: a new apartment building in Krásné Pole, Ostrava. The client describes it as:

> Krásné prostředí, příroda i město, vše na dosah, světlé a prostorné moderní byty.

(Beautiful surroundings, nature and the city both within reach; bright, spacious, modern apartments.) Use only these claims. Specific amenities, distances or transport links have not been supplied, so do not invent them.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **BUILD HAVEN s.r.o.** (written as "BuildHaven" in the project).
- Logo: a gold, line-drawn roof and buildings mark above "BUILD" in gold and "HAVEN" in white, with "s.r.o." between thin gold rules, all on black. The logo itself is the company's mark and must not be redrawn.
- The logo's black and gold is **not** a binding site palette. The client explicitly delegated the site's visual theme ("modern, simple, whatever you think is best"). The current logo file is `Logo.png`, a transparent PNG (1254×1254) with wide empty margins and a faint soft halo around the artwork. "HAVEN" and "s.r.o." are white, so as supplied it works only on dark backgrounds. On a light background those words disappear.
- **Style reference (client-chosen, 2026-10-23):** the client turned down distinctive concept directions and wants the conventional, smooth, modern developer-site look, specifically in the manner of https://www.findanreg.cz/ (deep petrol-navy grounds, warm sand accents, a clean geometric sans, slanted image crops, a stats row, calm premium tone). Match that site's craft level. Do not copy its content or claims. Also not wanted: template-generic, busy.
- **Decision (client-approved):** on the website, use the gold mark alone (`assets/logo-mark.png`) and set the company name "BuildHaven" / "BUILD HAVEN s.r.o." as live text, not as an image. The mark works on both light and dark backgrounds.

## Product Principles

1. **Availability first.** A buyer should reach the units, their layouts and sizes, and their availability within a few seconds.
2. **Trust through concreteness.** Real plans, real areas, a real company with real contact details. Never use vague promises or invented proof.
3. **Contact is always one step away.** Every apartment detail leads naturally to getting in touch.
4. **Location is the story.** Krásné Pole, where nature and the city are both within reach, is the differentiator. Present it only with the supplied claims.
5. **Plans can change.** Clearly mark study-stage data as indicative.
