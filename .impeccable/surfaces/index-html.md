# Surface brief: index.html (BuildHaven homepage, one-page site)

Scope: the whole public site, a single scrolling page in Czech. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: prospective buyers of the 8 new apartments in Krásné Pole, Ostrava.
Action: browse the 8 units (area, price rate, status, plans), then call or email. Phone and email links only, no form.
Proof on hand: real floor plans for 1.NP and 2.NP (assets/plans), logo mark, confirmed areas, price rate, availability date, contacts, company details. Visualisations arrive later; the slots are built ready for them.
Constraints: do not compute total prices; floor-per-unit mapping is unconfirmed, so do not state it; no invented amenities, distances, testimonials or stats.
Unresolved: unit-to-floor/plan mapping; visualisation images; company history.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard, executed at the craft of findanreg.cz (client-chosen canon). Calm, premium, smooth. It refuses the template tells: no rounded icon-card rows, no stock photos, no eyebrow labels.

OWN-WORLD: Deep petrol ink (#0E2A31) owns the hero, the closing band and the footer. A neutral light paper (#F2F3F0, cooled from warm #F5F2EC to match the canon reference's neutral light grounds) and white for the reading sections. Sand (#C4B49E) for buttons and highlighted words. The gold logo mark is the only gold. Urbanist geometric sans throughout. Square-cornered sand buttons. Slanted parallelogram image frames echo the roof angle of the logo mark. Faint plan linework as a texture on dark grounds.

STORY: The visitor learns: 8 new apartments in Krásné Pole, 79,58–123,73 m², 90 000 Kč/m², ready from February 2027, all available. They believe it because they can see the real plans and the real people. They pick a unit and call Marek or David.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Petrol hero, sticky header with the mark plus "BuildHaven" text and nav. Left: H1 "Nové byty v Krásném Poli", a supporting line, a sand "Vybrat byt" button and a phone link. Right: a slanted white frame holding the 1.NP plan (swaps to a visualisation later). Along the bottom edge, a facts strip: 8 bytů · 79,58–123,73 m² · 90 000 Kč/m² · od února 2027.

FORM: canon (category standard, client steered to findanreg.cz); position: chosen over the roll by the client. seed:da3da91cf78b1ae9. Signature interaction: the unit selector. Choosing a byt (row or deep link #byt-N) opens its detail panel with its area, rate, status and prefilled call and email actions. Motion is one orchestrated soft reveal on load plus the panel transition; reduced motion is respected.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
